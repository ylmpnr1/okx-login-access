## First-Time Login: What Happens After You Register

A new OKX account moves through three gates before it's fully usable.

**Step 1 — Email or phone + password.** You register with a phone number or email and a password that must be 8–32 characters with at least one lowercase, one uppercase, one number, and one symbol. After confirming, OKX sends a verification code to the same channel.

**Step 2 — KYC (identity verification).** OKX requires KYC before you can deposit, trade, or withdraw. There are two individual levels:

- **Level 1 (Basic):** Name, country, and a government-issued ID (passport, national ID, or driver's license). Unlocks deposits and limited trading.
- **Level 2 (Advanced):** Adds proof of address (utility bill or bank statement issued within the last 3 months) plus a selfie/liveness check. Unlocks higher withdrawal limits and full product access.

Without KYC, the account exists but can't move funds. With Level 1 only, the daily crypto withdrawal cap is typically $10,000 USD; Level 2 raises it to $1,000,000 USD; institutional Level 3 is custom. These limits also scale with your fee tier — see the table below.

**Step 3 — 2FA setup.** OKX strongly pushes you to bind at least one secondary verification method: phone, email, and/or a TOTP authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.). Two-factor authentication cannot be turned off once enabled — only reset or replaced.

## New-Device Authorization: The Step That Catches Most People

When you sign in from a device OKX hasn't seen before, the platform triggers an extra authorization step on top of password + 2FA. This is the single most common reason a working login suddenly fails after a phone upgrade or a trip.

OKX offers four verification paths, and which ones appear depends on whether you've completed KYC and what you've bound:

- **Phone verification:** OKX calls the registered number; you press 1 to authorize or 2 to cancel. Up to 5 calls per hour.
- **Email verification:** A confirmation link is sent to the registered email. Copy-paste the link if it doesn't open inline.
- **Authenticator app:** Enter the TOTP code from your linked authenticator.
- **Facial recognition:** Required on the app for accounts that have completed KYC. The camera compares your face to the ID on file — blink, nod, or shake your head when prompted.

Two important rules OKX publishes about this step:

> The verification code and the facial recognition check must be performed in the same IP network environment. If the network differs between web and app, complete the operation in the app for a smoother flow.

And:

> If the device has not logged in within 30 days of being recognized as a different device, you will need to log in again, and it will no longer be considered an authorizable device.

If every option fails, the app has a "Request manual verification" button and the web has a Customer Support route. There's no hard cap on the number of devices you can be logged in on simultaneously.

## Account Restricted or Frozen: What's Actually Going On

OKX distinguishes between two states, and the recovery path is different for each.

**Account restriction (self-initiated):** You or your emergency contact froze the account. You can reverse it by completing security authentication (phone, email, or authenticator).

**Account restriction (by OKX):** The risk system flagged your account. OKX lists four common triggers:

- Unusual or suspicious activity detected by the risk engine
- Additional information needed to continue servicing the account
- Periodic compliance review
- Confirmed Terms of Service violation

What you can still do while restricted: log in, view balances and history, close open positions, and submit requested documents. What's typically blocked: withdrawals, new trades, and deposits.

The appeal path depends on the trigger:

- **Risk-system flag:** Open a support ticket explaining the situation; manual review decides whether to lift.
- **Periodic compliance review:** Submit the requested documents through the Ticket Center or in-app banner. You can't skip this.
- **Abnormal login detection:** Complete identity re-verification in the Security Center, then contact support if still blocked.
- **Confirmed ToS violation (permanent):** No appeal. You keep the right to withdraw remaining crypto to an external wallet within the deadline stated in the closure email (commonly 14, 30, or 180 days).

For unfreezing, OKX typically asks for: a current government-issued photo ID, a proof-of-address document less than 3 months old, and a selfie or live video liveness check. Standard reviews complete in a few hours to one business day; enhanced reviews can take 1–3 business days. Blurry photos, expired IDs, and name mismatches with the OKX profile are the most common rejection reasons.

## Common Login Errors and Quick Fixes

Most "I can't log in" reports fall into a small number of buckets. Here's what to try in order, based on OKX's published troubleshooting guides.

**App won't load or keeps crashing.** Force-quit, restart your device, update the app from the official store, switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, disable any VPN or proxy, and clear cache (Android: Settings → Apps → OKX → Storage → Clear Cache; iOS: delete and reinstall). If you've created an OKX Web3 wallet, back up your seed phrase before uninstalling — losing it means losing the wallet's assets.

**Login page stuck on a spinner.** Same network and cache steps. Also check `okx.com/status` for platform outages — if the issue is on OKX's side, waiting is the only fix.

**"Account frozen" or "Action restricted" banner.** Don't keep retrying — repeated failed attempts can deepen the restriction. Check your registered email (including spam) for a notice from OKX, then go to the Ticket Center for the specific next step.

**2FA code not arriving.** For SMS: wait 60 seconds and request again; check that your phone has signal and isn't on a call-blocking plan. For email: check spam and promotions tabs; copy-paste the link if the button doesn't work. For authenticator app: make sure your phone's clock is set to automatic — TOTP codes depend on accurate time.

**Facial recognition fails on a new device.** Make sure the OKX app has camera and photo gallery permissions, update the app, switch network (try 4G/5G instead of Wi-Fi), and perform the check in a well-lit room with your full face visible. If it still fails, use the in-app **Request manual verification** option.

**Withdrawals disabled for 24 hours after a credential change.** This is by design, not a bug. OKX applies a 24-hour withdrawal hold after password changes, 2FA resets, phone number changes, and email changes. Plan accordingly if you're about to make a time-sensitive transfer.
